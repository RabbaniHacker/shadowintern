# 🗺️ ShadowIntern — Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Indian Tech Focus (MVP)
**Target:** Top 20 Indian tech companies
**Companies:** Razorpay, Zepto, Swiggy, CRED, PhonePe, Groww, Meesho, Zomato, Dunzo, BrowserStack, Freshworks, Chargebee, Postman, Setu, Juspay, Niyo, Slice, Jupiter, Perfios, Sarvam AI

**MVP features:**
- [ ] Company Intelligence Agent (GitHub + tech blog only)
- [ ] Basic Culture Modeller
- [ ] Task Generator (1 company: Razorpay)
- [ ] Shadow Mentor (code review only)
- [ ] Simple web dashboard

**Success metric:** 100 students complete a 4-week shadow and rate preparation quality ≥ 4/5

---

## Phase 2 — Global Expansion
**Target:** Any company with sufficient public data

**New features:**
- [ ] Multi-source scraper (Glassdoor, LinkedIn, YouTube)
- [ ] Automated company onboarding (no manual config)
- [ ] Mobile dashboard
- [ ] Interview Simulator (Round 1: DSA)

---

## Phase 3 — Monetisation
**Model:** ₹500/month per company shadow

**Features:**
- [ ] Full Interview Simulator (all 3 rounds)
- [ ] Progress tracking + portfolio generation
- [ ] "Shadow Certificate" on completion
- [ ] Referral program

**Target:** 1 lakh students = ₹5 Cr/month ARR

---

## Phase 4 — B2B / Reverse Model
**Model:** Companies pay ShadowIntern to send pre-trained candidates

**Features:**
- [ ] Company partner portal
- [ ] Candidate scoring visible to hiring managers
- [ ] Custom task bank built with company's engineering team
- [ ] Pre-screened candidate pipeline product
